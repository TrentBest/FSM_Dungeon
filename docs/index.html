A Workshop world-simulation proof

A dungeon with a reason to exist.

Not a random stack of rooms and monsters. A place shaped by its origin, history, purpose, inhabitants, and the things that have happened there.

Foundation in progress · deterministic proof is next

One world. Multiple ways to experience it.

FSM_Dungeon is being built as a reusable .NET domain and simulation library. This site will become its public proof surface: inspect generated spaces, follow their provenance, understand inhabitants, and eventually explore the world through an original isometric action-RPG experience.

CauseWhy the place formed: natural, constructed, biological, industrial, ancient, or something not yet understood.
LifeWhy inhabitants are here: territory, orders, shelter, hunting, work, worship, maintenance, or survival.
ObservationHow much of the world needs to be computed and shown for this observer, intent, and budget.

The first playable proof

  1. A small sanctuary with a discoverable descent.
  2. A seeded, connected dungeon whose rooms retain causal provenance.
  3. Distinct inhabitant groups with goals and meaningful territory.
  4. Exploration, real-time combat, danger, and a return loop.
  5. Original loot and persistent character growth.
  6. An inspectable world model and automated invariants.

Built with Workshop boundaries

ProtocolAi gives domain meaning deterministic identities. GrammarAi describes valid structures. CommandAi will carry proposals through host validation. Renderer selects computational detail for an observer and budget. Visualization turns selected representations into richer visuals. WebApp and ServerApp can host the same domain without owning it.

The domain remains useful without an AI model, renderer, browser, server, or game engine.
